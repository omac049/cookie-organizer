    Getting Started

    Cookie Organizer helps you manage and analyze browser cookies with a user-friendly interface. Let's take a quick tour of the main features.

    2

    Scanning Cookies

    Click the Scan Cookies button to examine all cookies from the current site. The extension will categorize them automatically.

    3

    Cookie Categories

    Cookies are organized by their purpose (necessary, preferences, analytics, marketing, social) and by their relationship to the site (primary, secondary, third-party).

    Managing Cookies

    You can view, edit, or delete individual cookies. Click the Edit button to modify a cookie's value, expiration, or security settings.

    5

    Bulk Actions

    Select multiple cookies using the checkboxes to perform actions on them as a group. You can select all visible cookies or delete multiple cookies at once.

    Select All 0 selected
    6

    Advanced Features

    Cookie Organizer includes additional capabilities:

    • Export Cookies - Download in JSON, cURL, or Netscape formats
    • Data Visualization - View charts summarizing your cookie data
    • Value Decoder - Automatically detect and decode JWT tokens, Base64, and URL-encoded values
    • Advanced Filtering - Filter cookies by type, purpose, or relationship
